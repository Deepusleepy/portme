# Phase 2 — Stress-test rules

**Prerequisites.** Phase 1 approval matched to the actual conversation; frozen manifest/check-input authorization bundle digest, exact checks, and check-input declarations explicitly authorized; approved model plan and triggered rulebook.
**Inputs.** Risk-ranked manifest, contract rows, applicable map/gaps/rules, judge, production packet, cost log, `templates/cost-log.tsv`.
**Permitted mutations.** Implement one isolated disposable or kept-in-place slice; change judge only under independent review, with sensitivity mutations confined to a disposable copy/worktree/snapshot and unchanged-original receipts; queue artifact amendments for user approval.
**Outputs.** Slice evidence, adversarial findings, keep/discard receipt, proposed amendments, corrected map/gaps, measured cost, Phase 3 parameters.
**Gate evidence.** Judge and mutation results, reviewer-selected attacks, material amendments, disposal proof, affected units, forecast and raw paths.
**Stop condition.** Set `awaiting-gate-signoff` and stop; reclassify unprovable isolation, and block on unresolved high-risk findings or material amendments.
